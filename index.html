What real AI will check

Ingredient recognition

Suggest likely food names and categories; ask you to confirm.

Visible quality signals

Flag visible mold-like growth, bruising or discoloration for human review—not a safety verdict.

Nutrition lookup

Match confirmed foods to a verified nutrition database with portion size and source shown.

Safety limitation

Vision cannot reliably detect pathogens, toxins or unsafe storage history.

Nutrition totals

Calculated from your entered food values and meal log. Inventory totals are not the same as what you ate.

How to improve accuracy

Manual check

Use a dedicated appliance thermometer and follow local food-safety guidance. Do not rely on this demo for temperature monitoring.

Sensor integration roadmap

Temperature sensor

Bluetooth or Wi-Fi sensor → secure gateway → timestamped readings and alerts.

Door / camera events

Optional door-open events and periodic shelf photos with explicit consent.

Alerts

Notify when a configured temperature threshold is exceeded for a sustained period.

Not a safety guarantee

A normal temperature reading does not prove food is safe; time, handling and contamination also matter.
